# Namma Metro Tracker v8.2

Customer-facing redesign focused on commuter tasks.

## Primary pages
- Home: journey planning and quick access.
- Your Journey: route, fare, duration, next-train estimate and service times.
- Metro Map: full Bengaluru schematic-style network with route focus and moving train markers.
- Live Trains: customer-facing moving train view.

## Design
Brand palette:
- #2FF2CA
- #F2C072
- #7884D9
- #14A7CF

Developer/backend concepts are intentionally kept out of the customer UI.
